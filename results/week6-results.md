## Caveats on these numbers

- **Single run, not a reproducible one.** Groq is not bit-deterministic even at temperature 0, so a re-run can shift a verdict or two. Every figure here comes from one recorded run, and the raw model responses are kept in `eval/raw/judge_*_run.json`.
- **25 cases is a small denominator.** One flipped verdict moves agreement by 4%. A move of that size is not signal.
- **judge_v2 saw two of these cases as examples.** They stay inside the headline denominator, because excluding them would be self-scoring. `results/week6/disagreements.md` reports agreement over the non-few-shot cases separately, which is the number that shows what generalised.
